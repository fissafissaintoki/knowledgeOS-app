## 1. Short Description

Prompterator converts rough input into structured prompts, workflows, and reusable work artifacts.

It helps users turn unclear ideas, tasks, notes, and requirements into clear AI instructions and operational outputs.

---

## 2. What Problem It Solves

Most people do not fail because they lack ideas.

They fail because their input is unstructured:

- vague task descriptions
- unclear goals
- missing constraints
- no output format
- no quality check
- no reuse logic

Prompterator solves this by turning raw input into structured AI-ready work packages.

---

## 3. Core Promise

```text
Raw thought in.
Structured work artifact out.
```

Prompterator helps convert:

- rough ideas into prompts
- tasks into workflows
- requirements into use cases
- process problems into SOP drafts
- concepts into Codex or Claude handoff prompts
- notes into reusable knowledge artifacts

---

## 4. How It Works

Prompterator follows a simple flow:

```text
Input
  ↓
Clarify intent
  ↓
Detect problem class
  ↓
Select output mode
  ↓
Generate structured artifact
  ↓
Add quality check
  ↓
Prepare for copy, reuse, or export
```

## 6. Who It Is For

Prompterator is for people who use AI seriously for work, planning, documentation, prototyping, or process design.

Useful for:

- operators
- founders
- consultants
- process thinkers
- logistics and operations teams
- creators
- AI power users
- people who want better prompts without learning prompt engineering from scratch

---

## 7. What It Is Not

Prompterator is not:

- an autonomous decision-maker
- a replacement for human judgment
- a compliance-certified enterprise system by default
- a tool that guarantees perfect outputs
- a system that controls other tools without human approval

Prompterator structures work. The human user remains responsible for decisions, validation, and final use.
